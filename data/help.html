Browser App

Overview

mORS is a Chrome/Edge extension that provides an overlay for the Oregon Revised Statutes (the codified laws of the state of Oregon) on the Oregon legislature's website to make the text easier to read and navigate. It also improves navigation to Oregon's session laws.

Help Using Search

There are three types of searches that the app allows you to perform from your browser's search bar (sometimes called an address bar or omnibox). To use the app, each shortcut begins with "ors" and a space:

1. ORS Chapters

Type 'ors' + space + the chapter into browser search bar:

ors 86A or: ors 009

2. ORS Sections

Type 'ors ' + section number to be taken right to the section:

ors 97.200 or: ors 72a.3070

3. Oregon Session Laws

Full text of the entire law (adapted from the enrolled and signed bill).

Type the year + "c." (most abbreviations for chapter should work) + the chapter. E.g:

ors 2019 c.1 ⇒ chapter 1, Oregon Laws 2019

Includes special sessions:

ors 2020 s.s.1 chp. 13 ⇒ chapter 13, Oregon Laws 2020 (first special session)

For session law, you can also switch reader from the default in the popup:

Use "hein" for HeinOnline*, e.g.:

ors hein 2005 c.550

Use "orLeg" for OregonLegislature.gov**, e.g.:

ors orleg 2014 c.501

* HeinOnline requires a subscription and defaults to a login through the State Law Library of Oregon. From 1849 to 2024.

** Session law on the Oregon Legislature website currently goes from 1999 to 2025 (regular session).

Notes

You can type a string a valid terms separated by most non alphanumeric character (not a period). E.g.,:

ors 9.460 ; 701 / 2019 c.444, hein 1989 ch. 658

...will launch four tabs ⇒

  • ORS 9.460
  • ORS chapter 701
  • chapter 444, Oregon Laws 2019
  • chapter 658, Oregon Laws 1989 (in HeinOnline)

If something seems broken, email mORS.Development@gmail.com or post an issue to GitHub.

Privacy Policy

mORS respects your privacy. This policy explains our data collection and storage practices.

Your person extension preferences (set in the popup menu) for the mORS browser application are stored using your browser's sync storage. This data:

  • May be shared in between browsers if your browser is logged into an account;
  • Syncs across your devices where you're signed into that account
  • Is never transmitted to our servers;
  • Is never shared with third parties;
  • Can be deleted by uninstalling the extension

No other data is generated for or by this application.

We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.
